# Economic & Innovation Digital Twin Research Portfolio

## Portfolio taxonomy

**27 independent twin specifications:** seven existing financial/banking twins (in [projects/](projects/)) plus twenty economic, market, innovation and policy twins in the five thematic domain directories. The new `financial-and-banking-twins/` folder is an index, **not seven duplicate specifications**. The shared platform and four foundations are **not counted as projects**.

## Delivery strategy

**Foundation phase:** ontology, units, vintages, stock-flow ledger, scenario and validation contracts. **Pilot:** technology diffusion + labor skills + AI transformation with public data and synthetic firms. **Integration:** macro output/household demand, then merchant credit/payments where authorized. **Expansion:** pricing games, policy counterfactuals and multi-domain stress.

## Research quality criteria

A twin must declare observability, calibration data, uncertainty, model alternatives, invariants, causal limitations, computational convergence and explicit decision utility. Otherwise label it simulation prototype. No synthetic result is evidence of real-world predictive power.
