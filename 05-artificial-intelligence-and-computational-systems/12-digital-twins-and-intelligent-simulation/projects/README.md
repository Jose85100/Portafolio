# Digital Twins — seven independent research and engineering projects

Seven **S1 specifications**, not seven implemented simulators. Shared [foundations](../fundamentals/README.md), [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [contracts](../DATA_CONTRACTS.md) and [validation protocol](../VALIDATION_AND_ROADMAP.md).

- [Digital Banking System Twin](01-digital-banking-system-twin/) — Digital bank economics, deposit behavior and balance-sheet feedback
- [Real-Time Payments & Settlement Twin](02-real-time-payments-settlement-twin/) — Payment rails, queueing, settlement and network liquidity
- [Credit Portfolio & Collections Twin](03-credit-portfolio-collections-twin/) — Credit states, cashflows, prepayment, collections and loss
- [Liquidity, Treasury & ALM Twin](04-liquidity-treasury-alm-twin/) — Liquidity buffers, deposit outflows, collateral, funding and rate shocks
- [Fraud & Financial Crime Twin](05-fraud-financial-crime-twin/) — Adaptive fraud attacks, AML alert capacity and customer friction
- [Operational & Cyber Resilience Twin](06-operational-cyber-resilience-twin/) — Service dependencies, outage cascades and recovery
- [Integrated Financial Contagion & Stress Twin](07-integrated-financial-contagion-stress-twin/) — Cross-domain contagion, reverse stress and decision support
