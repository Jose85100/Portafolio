# Statistical reliability and performance

Define estimands: p95/p99 response latency, throughput at fixed error rate, availability over observation window, mean time to recovery, task completion, retrieval recall and security false accept rate. Quantile confidence intervals via resampling independent load-test runs or dependence-aware bootstrap; do not naively bootstrap correlated individual requests. Compare Kotlin vs Rust on matched hardware, workload, serialization, connection pooling and measurement method. Model arrival burstiness, long-tail dependencies and correlated outages; estimate error budgets and detect drift with sequential monitoring. For RAG evaluate citation faithfulness and ACL leakage separately from answer fluency. For AI-assisted development run controlled studies of defect escape and time saved; report confidence intervals and review bias.
