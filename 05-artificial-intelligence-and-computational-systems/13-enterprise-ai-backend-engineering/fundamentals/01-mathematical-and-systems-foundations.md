# Mathematical and distributed-systems foundations

Queue stability: for stationary arrival λ and mean service μ across c workers, ρ=λ/(cμ)<1 is necessary for a simple work-conserving homogeneous queue, not a sufficient production SLO. Little's law L=λW under steady-state conditions. Analyze tail latency, retries as arrival amplification and finite buffer overflow. For DAG workflows, critical-path bound T≥max_path Σd_v; scheduling with heterogeneous workers and precedence constraints is generally combinatorial. Model durable workflows as transition systems and prove invariants: no invalid state transitions, no double financial side effects, monotone sequence/version and eventual completion only under explicit liveness assumptions. Distinguish consensus, message delivery and transaction atomicity. A broker alone does not guarantee exactly-once external effects.
